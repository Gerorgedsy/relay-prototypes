Relay Prototypes

Team #3 · A worry tracker for bedside nurses. Three prototypes, three hypotheses. In each one you're the same night nurse, and you type and save a worry about a patient.

1 · Relay SimpleOne Worried button in the EHR. Type it, save it, other nurses see it. 2 · Relay TeamAsk a teammate for a second look, and help with a teammate's worry. 3 · Relay TogetherThe simple EHR button, plus the charge nurse and the next nurse at handoff.
